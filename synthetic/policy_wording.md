TRAVELSAFE INSURANCE - TRAVEL POLICY WORDING TRV-2026 (SYNTHETIC, FOR SIMULATION ONLY)

Section 1 - About this policy
1.1 How to read this policy. This wording, together with your policy schedule, forms your contract. Words in the schedule such as plan, limits and excess apply to every section. All amounts are in US dollars (USD).
1.2 Schedule of benefits. Standard plan: travel delay expenses up to USD 500; baggage delay essentials up to USD 300; baggage loss up to USD 2,000 in total, single item limit USD 500, valuables combined limit USD 400, baggage excess USD 50 per claim. Premier plan: travel delay expenses up to USD 1,000; baggage delay essentials up to USD 500; baggage loss up to USD 3,000 in total, single item limit USD 750, valuables combined limit USD 800, no baggage excess.

Section 2 - Who and when you are covered
2.1 Period of cover. Cover applies only to journeys that start on or after the policy start date and on or before the policy end date shown in the schedule. No benefit is payable for any event that happens outside the period of cover.
2.2 Insured persons. Only persons named as insured persons on the policy schedule may claim. Claims by or for persons not named on the schedule are not covered.

Section 3 - Travel delay
3.1 Travel delay benefit. If the departure of your booked flight is delayed by at least 6 hours, we reimburse reasonable additional expenses you necessarily incur for meals, refreshments, accommodation and local transport while waiting for the delayed departure.
3.2 Covered causes of delay. The delay must be caused by adverse weather, mechanical breakdown of the aircraft, air traffic control restrictions, or industrial action that was not publicly announced before you booked your trip.
3.3 Delay exclusions. We do not pay for delays of less than 6 hours, for delays caused by industrial action or strikes that were publicly announced before your booking date, or if you did not check in on time.
3.4 Compensation offset. Any compensation, refund or voucher paid to you by the airline or another provider for the same delay is deducted from the amount we pay.
3.5 Non-essential expenses. We do not pay for non-essential expenses such as alcohol, spa or wellness treatments, entertainment, shopping or upgrades, because these are not necessary costs caused by the delay.
3.6 Travel delay limit. The most we pay for all travel delay expenses for one delayed journey is the travel delay limit shown in your schedule.

Section 4 - Baggage delay
4.1 Baggage delay benefit. If your checked baggage is delayed on your outward journey by more than 12 hours, we reimburse the cost of essential replacement items such as toiletries and clothing that you buy before your baggage is returned. No benefit is payable if the baggage is returned within 12 hours.
4.2 Property irregularity report. You must report the delay to the airline and obtain a property irregularity report (PIR) at the airport.
4.3 Baggage delay limit. The most we pay for baggage delay is the baggage delay limit shown in your schedule.

Section 5 - Baggage loss
5.1 Baggage loss benefit. If your baggage or personal possessions are lost, stolen or damaged during your journey, we pay the value of the items at the time of loss, up to the limits in your schedule.
5.2 Single item limit. The most we pay for any one item, pair or set of items is the single item limit shown in your schedule.
5.3 Valuables limit. The most we pay in total for valuables, meaning jewellery, watches, cameras, electronic devices and items made of precious metals, is the valuables limit shown in your schedule.
5.4 Wear, tear and depreciation. Items older than one year are reduced for wear, tear and loss of value by 10 percent for each full year since purchase, up to a maximum reduction of 50 percent.
5.5 Property irregularity report for loss. Loss of checked baggage must be reported to the airline within 24 hours and supported by a property irregularity report. A claim without a PIR is incomplete until it is provided.
5.6 Baggage excess. The baggage excess shown in your schedule is deducted from each baggage loss claim.
5.7 Baggage loss limit. The most we pay for all baggage loss under one claim is the baggage loss limit shown in your schedule.

Section 6 - Evidence and payment
6.1 Receipts and proof of purchase. You must provide an original receipt or proof of purchase for any item or expense above USD 150. We do not pay for items or expenses above USD 150 without this evidence.
6.2 Currency conversion. Amounts in another currency are converted to USD at the policy exchange rate on the date of the loss or expense, not at the rate you used.
6.3 Excluded items. We do not pay for cash, travel tickets, passports and travel documents, perishable goods, or items left unattended in a public place.
6.4 Payment. We pay approved amounts by bank transfer to the account of the insured person who made the claim.

Section 7 - General conditions
7.1 Fraud. If a claim is fraudulent or exaggerated, we will not pay it and may cancel the policy.
7.2 Duplicate claims. We do not pay twice for the same loss or expense. A claim for an event already claimed under this or another policy is not paid again.

Section 8 - Complaints and appeals
8.1 Appeals. If you disagree with our decision you may ask for it to be reviewed by a claims specialist within 30 days of our letter. If you are still not satisfied you may refer your complaint to the ombudsman service.
