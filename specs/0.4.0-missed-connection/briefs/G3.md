## What Changed

Built from `tasks.md` (55 of 56 tasks done; T043 Compliance pending for G4):

- **Claim type** `missed_connection` through the existing agents; routing, limits, confidence weights
  and the payment path unchanged.
- **Deterministic code**: inbound leg matching, minimum connection time from
  `reference/minimum-connection-times.yaml` (values confirmed by `po`), single-ticket check,
  arrival-delay verification against the flight-status source (which now returns arrival times),
  journey shared limit and exact-match duplicate receipts (7.2, type A), unused-ticket refund offset.
- **Routing outcome by design** (research R8): unverified inbound delay → confidence ≤ 0.35 → row 6;
  short connection, self-transfer, excluded cause, late check-in → rejection → row 5. A test asserts no
  missed connection reaches rows 1-3 without a verified delay, valid connection and single ticket.
- **Letters**: three missed connection templates drafted, status `pending_compliance`; until approved
  (G4) missed connection claims use the approved generic decision letters.
- **Evals**: 4 new metrics for the G1 thresholds, measured on 15 labelled golden cases; 4 new
  adversarial cases (ADV-005..008).
- **Ground truth**: golden cases labelled by `l2-grace`, reviewed by `hoc`; adversarial by `ailead`
  with `l2-grace` (T047/T048, 2026-11-02). One correction after labelling: the four rejected cases now
  carry their rejection clause (3.3 / 3.7, as shown in the labelled table); without it coverage
  agreement read 0.8788.
