## What Changed

Release 0.4.0 widens the existing use case with a missed connection benefit (CARD-101):

- Problem and proposed solution extended: missed onward connections caused by a delayed inbound flight
  (covered cause, clause 3.2) are paid under a new clause 3.7 (rebooking, accommodation, meals), with
  travel delay's exclusions and compensation offset (clause 3.4).
- Value hypothesis: +3 metrics (complaints, goodwill payments outside the system, decision time).
- Scope in: missed connection (Standard and Premier, flights only), documents for both legs, inbound
  delay verified with the flight-status tool, a new customer letter. "Missed connection" removed from
  scope out.
- Scope out (new): rail/ferry/coach connections, self-transfer on separate tickets [to be confirmed],
  any change to routing, straight-through limit or approval authority.
- Unchanged: data classification (no new data categories), risk answers and tier (HIGH),
  feasibility verdict and conditions, RACI.
