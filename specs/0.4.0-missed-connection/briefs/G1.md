## What Changed

Requirements for the missed connection benefit, from `spec.md` "ADLC: G1 Requirements":

- **Requirements** (`docs/requirements/`): REQ-F-001 amended (new claim type; combined delay + missed
  connection stays one claim); new REQ-F-032 to REQ-F-039 (completeness, legs, inbound delay
  verification, minimum connection time, eligibility, calculation, double payment, letter) and
  REQ-B-012 / REQ-B-013 (never pay without verified delay and valid connection; never attribute the
  delay to the wrong leg). 45 requirements in total, each with a verification method.
- **KPIs** (`docs/kpis.yaml`): KPI-07 to KPI-09 from the card; targets kept as the card's figures,
  baselines still placeholders.
- **Eval thresholds** (`evals/thresholds.yaml`): +4: `missed_connection_eligibility_accuracy` ≥ 0.95,
  `connection_time_check_accuracy` = 1.0, `inbound_leg_attribution_accuracy` = 1.0,
  `duplicate_expense_paid_count` = 0. Existing 23 unchanged.
- **Authority**: no change. Routing matrix rows 1-7, USD 1,000 straight-through limit, 25%
  significance, 0.7 confidence, HITL points and the payment approval token all unchanged.
- **Business decisions recorded in the spec** (Clarifications, 2026-11-02, answered by `hoc`):
  self-transfer on separate tickets is out; travel delay and missed connection share one travel
  delay limit per journey (USD 500 Standard / USD 1,000 Premier); outward and return are separate
  journeys; minimum connection time is the airport's published value, 60 minutes fallback; no
  minimum inbound delay; no separate hotel / meal caps (as travel delay); refunds for the unused
  onward ticket are offset; a combined delay + missed connection claim stays one claim.

Requirement text was refined after the Business owner's G1 confirmation (clarifications above); the
confirmed limits, KPI targets and thresholds did not change.
