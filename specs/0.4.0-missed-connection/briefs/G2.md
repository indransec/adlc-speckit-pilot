## What Changed

Design for the missed connection benefit, from `plan.md` "ADLC: G2 Design Record" and
`research.md`:

- **No new agent, tool, model or dependency.** The new claim type runs through intake, document,
  coverage, verification, assessment and communication; topology and delegation unchanged.
- **Authority unchanged**: `routing.py`, routing matrix, system.yaml routing keys, confidence weights
  and the approval token are untouched. Failures reach a person through existing rules: an
  unverified inbound delay sets the verification signal to 0, capping confidence at 0.35 → row 6
  investigation; a short connection, self-transfer or excluded cause becomes a rejection → row 5
  (L1 + L2) (research R8).
- **Deterministic code** for the safety-relevant steps: inbound leg matching (R2), minimum
  connection time check (R4, ADR-006), single-ticket check, shared journey limit and duplicate
  receipts (R5-R7, ADR-007). The model extracts and matches clauses only.
- **Tool contracts** (Security): TOOL-FLIGHT-STATUS `lookup` output adds `scheduled_arr`,
  `actual_arr`, `arrival_delay_minutes` (same op, read, verification only); TOOL-CLAIMS-SYSTEM new
  read op `journey_benefits` for orchestrator code, not attached to any model.
- **Threat model**: T10 wrong/stale MCT, T11 delay on the wrong leg, T12 double payment across
  claims, T13 altered itinerary or boarding-pass times, each with a control and a verification.
- **Data**: boarding-pass and itinerary fields stay in "Travel documents" (Confidential); barcode not
  decoded; booking references redacted in logs; no new data category (R10).
- **Change control**: new `reference/` directory owned by Business owner + AI lead (CODEOWNERS).
