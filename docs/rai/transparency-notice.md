# Customer transparency notice (draft, for Compliance review)

We use automated tools, including AI, to help process your claim faster. These tools
read your documents, check your policy cover and calculate the amount payable. For a missed connection we
also check your flights against airline flight data and the airport's minimum connection time.
Simple claims may be approved automatically. A claims specialist reviews any claim
where we propose to pay less than you claimed or to decline it. If you disagree with
a decision you can appeal, and a person will review your claim.
